About Engineering AI

Engineering AI is an AI-powered engineering and technology assistant developed to support learning, technical exploration, problem-solving, and engineering calculations. It brings engineering knowledge and artificial intelligence together to make complex technical topics easier to understand.

Our mission

Our mission is to make engineering knowledge more accessible through intelligent technology, understandable explanations, and practical digital tools that support students, learners, and engineering professionals.

What Engineering AI offers

  • Engineering calculations: Assistance with formulas, numerical problems, units, and calculation steps.
  • Technical explanations: Clear discussions of engineering principles and concepts.
  • Engineering exploration: Support for learning about petroleum, mechanical, electrical, civil, chemical, and computer engineering.
  • AI-powered assistance: A conversational workspace for asking technical questions and exploring possible solutions.

Our vision

We envision a future where accessible engineering technology helps people learn, explore ideas, develop solutions, and participate in innovation across multiple engineering fields.

Our commitment to responsible engineering

Engineering AI is designed to assist with learning and preliminary technical analysis. AI-generated answers and calculations may contain errors and depend on the accuracy of the supplied information, units, assumptions, and methods. Always verify important results against applicable standards, trusted references, and qualified engineering judgment before using them in real-world designs or operations.
