A PRODUCT BY EXCELLENT INTELLIGENCE

Engineering Intelligence. Made Accessible.

Engineering AI is an AI-powered engineering and technology assistant built to help people understand technical concepts, explore engineering problems, and perform calculations with greater clarity. By combining artificial intelligence with engineering knowledge, we aim to make technical learning and problem-solving more accessible.

Our mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between complex engineering knowledge and practical understanding. We develop intelligent digital tools that help students, learners, technology enthusiasts, and engineering professionals explore ideas, understand calculations, and approach technical challenges with greater confidence.

What Engineering AI can help you do

  • Engineering calculations: Work through formulas, numerical problems, units, assumptions, and calculation steps.
  • Technical learning: Explore engineering principles through clear explanations and structured problem-solving.
  • Multidisciplinary exploration: Investigate topics in petroleum, mechanical, electrical, civil, chemical, and computer engineering, alongside broader engineering technology.
  • AI-powered assistance: Ask technical questions, examine possible approaches, and use a conversational workspace to develop your understanding.
  • Continuous development: Benefit from a platform designed to grow as new engineering tools and learning capabilities are developed.

Built for learning and innovation

We believe that technology should make knowledge easier to explore. Engineering AI is being developed around that idea: combining accessible explanations, practical tools, and artificial intelligence in one workspace for technical discovery and problem-solving.

Our vision

Our vision is to build a trusted engineering technology platform that empowers people to learn, experiment with ideas, investigate technical challenges, and contribute to innovation across engineering disciplines.

Our commitment to responsible engineering

Engineering AI is an assistance tool for education, exploration, and preliminary technical analysis. AI-generated responses and calculations can contain errors and may depend on the quality of the supplied information, units, assumptions, and calculation methods.

Always independently verify important results using trusted technical references, applicable standards, appropriate validation methods, and qualified engineering review before applying them to real-world designs or operations.
